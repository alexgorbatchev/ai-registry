What changed in Claude threads?

Across 97 earlier and 155 later final replies, mean length fell from 334 to 300 words and the median from 332 to 280. Replies containing any bold ID rose from 68% to 70%. That count does not establish whether an ID identifies the right item.

Six threads had replies on both sides of the earlier prompt change. Their pooled mean fell from 443 to 355 words. Four of the six got shorter. We could not verify which instructions each session received, so the reduction cannot be attributed to the prompt. Sampling and field observations

How to read the scores

Each count shows replies passing every automatic check out of replies recorded. Each bar spans all planned replies. Hatching marks missing replies. Length is the mean whitespace word count. A brief answer can still miss a required fact or put the wrong ID on a finding. @@REUSED@@
